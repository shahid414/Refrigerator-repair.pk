What Our Refrigerator Mechanic Can Fix

From compact undercounter drink chillers to built-in 48-inch luxury French door systems, our refrigerator technicians tackle the most demanding mechanical and electrical breakdowns:

Sealed System & Compressor Overhauls

EPA-certified recovery, filter drier replacement, micro-leak ultraviolet detection, and precision electronic vacuum recharging.

Main Control Board (PCB) Diagnosis

Testing voltage outputs, decoding manufacturer error codes (e.g. 22E, 25E, Er FF), and soldering or replacing faulty power supply boards.

Defrost & Evaporator Thermal Systems

Restoring defrost bimetal heaters, thermistors, temperature controls, and fan motor harnesses to stop frost and ice buildup.

Water Dispenser & Dual Valves

Clearing frozen fill lines, replacing solenoid valve assemblies, and rebuilding automatic ice ejector mechanisms.
