Diagnostic Insight

Common Refrigerator Problems in Englewood

Colorado’s climate, high elevation, and local municipal water characteristics present specific mechanical stresses on refrigeration sealed loops and ice components.

! Clicking Sound Every Few Minutes with No Cooling

Why It Happens Here

In older Englewood residences, voltage drops or failing start relays cause the compressor to draw high locked-rotor amps, tripping the thermal overload switch.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We test compressor winding continuity, replace damaged start devices with OEM solid-state components, and verify steady operating amperage.

! Ice Buildup on Freezer Floor Leaking into Kitchen

Why It Happens Here

The defrost drain trough beneath the evaporator coil freezes over during cold winter months, causing daily defrost meltwater to run inside the cabinet.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We thaw the drain trough with controlled low-pressure steam, install a specialized copper drain heater probe, and verify unobstructed drainage.

! Loud Humming Noise That Stops When Door Opens

Why It Happens Here

The evaporator fan motor located inside the freezer compartment has worn bearings or is vibrating against ice encroaching from a defrost failure.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We inspect the evaporator fan blades, lubricate or replace the motor with factory-spec components, and clear any ice obstructions.

! Food Freezing in the Fresh Food Compartment

Why It Happens Here

A stuck thermistor or defective mechanical cold control fails to signal the damper to close, continuously blowing zero-degree air over produce and milk.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We test resistance values against temperature charts, replace the faulty temperature sensor or cold control thermostat, and calibrate airflow.
