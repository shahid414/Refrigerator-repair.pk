When To Call

Signs you need refrigerator repair

Recognizing the signs that you need refrigerator repair in Ken Caryl can save you time and money. If your appliance shows any of these symptoms, it's time to call a professional.

1. Refrigerator not cooling

If your refrigerator is warm inside, it may be due to a malfunctioning compressor or refrigerant leak. This requires immediate attention to prevent food spoilage.

2. Water pooling on the floor

A leak can indicate a clogged drain or damaged door seal. This issue can lead to water damage and mold growth if not addressed quickly.

3. Unusual noises coming from the fridge

Strange sounds could signal a failing compressor or fan. Ignoring these noises may lead to more significant issues and costly repairs.

4. Frost build-up in the freezer

Excessive frost can indicate a problem with the defrost system or door seals. This can affect the freezer's efficiency and require professional repair.

5. Ice maker not functioning

If your ice maker isn't producing ice, it may be due to a blocked water line or a malfunctioning component. Timely repairs can restore your ice supply quickly.
