Diagnostic Insight

Common Refrigerator Problems in Centennial

Colorado’s climate, high elevation, and local municipal water characteristics present specific mechanical stresses on refrigeration sealed loops and ice components.

! Ice Dispenser Makes Buzzing Sound but No Ice Comes Out

Why It Happens Here

In busy Centennial households, ice cubes frequently melt slightly and freeze together, jamming the motorized dispenser auger and stalling the motor.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We clear the ice chute, test the auger drive motor and solenoid actuator, and inspect the bin assembly for damaged drive pins.

! Refrigerator Constantly Running and Never Shuts Off

Why It Happens Here

Dust and pet hair accumulate on the underside condenser coil, preventing heat dissipation and forcing the compressor to run 24 hours a day.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We perform a deep commercial coil clean, inspect the condenser fan motor RPM, and test sealed system refrigerant pressure.

! Freezer Section Cold, Fresh Food Section at 50°F

Why It Happens Here

A failed electronic defrost control board or burned-out defrost heater allows thick frost to block the airflow damper connecting compartments.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We test defrost heater resistance with a digital multimeter, check the bimetal thermostat, and replace defective parts with OEM replacements.

! Puddle of Water Under the Front of the Refrigerator

Why It Happens Here

The secondary water valve solenoid feeding the in-door water dispenser develops a micro-crack or loose compression fitting under standard household water pressure.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We isolate the water supply, replace the dual-solenoid valve assembly with reinforced tubing, and leak-test under full line pressure.
