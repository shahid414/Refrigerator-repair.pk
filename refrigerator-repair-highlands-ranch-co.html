Common Refrigerator Problems in Highlands Ranch

Colorado’s climate, high elevation, and local municipal water characteristics present specific mechanical stresses on refrigeration sealed loops and ice components.

! Refrigerator Warm While Freezer Is Freezing

Why It Happens Here

In Highlands Ranch's dry climate, the motorized air diffuser or evaporator fan motor frequently fails, preventing chilled air from circulating from the freezer into the fresh food compartment.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We test the thermistor circuit, verify motorized damper opening angles, and inspect evaporator coil frost patterns to resolve airflow blockage.

! Hollow, Undersized, or Missing Ice Cubes

Why It Happens Here

Mineral scale buildup in water inlet valves coupled with sudden seasonal pressure changes throttles water fill time into the ice mold.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We check water line pressure, test solenoid coils with a digital multimeter, and replace clogged water filter assemblies with genuine OEM parts.

! Loud Clicking Sound and Compressor Shuts Off

Why It Happens Here

Summer heat spikes combined with high elevation cause compressor start relays and run capacitors to overheat and fail prematurely.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We test compressor winding resistance, inspect the PTC start relay for scorching, and install heavy-duty start components to restore reliable cooling.

! Water Puddling Beneath Bottom Freezer Drawers

Why It Happens Here

Dust and pet hair drawn into the bottom mechanical cabinet plug the duckbill defrost drain valve, forcing melted frost to freeze into an ice sheet that overflows onto kitchen floors.

Our Diagnostic Solution

We steam-thaw frozen drain gutters, modify or replace restrictive drain grommets, and verify clean drainage directly into the condensation pan.

How We Solve Highlands Ranch's Specific Appliance Challenges

High Elevation & Thinner Air Flow (5,920 ft)

At nearly 6,000 feet, reduced air density reduces convective heat transfer across condenser coils, causing compressors to run longer cycles and accumulate pet dander faster.

Solution: We conduct high-volume vacuum coil cleanings, evaluate condenser fan cubic-feet-per-minute (CFM) output, and test compressor operating amperage against high-altitude performance curves.

Hard Water Mineral Scaling in Ice Solenoids

Municipal water across southern Douglas County contains dissolved calcium and magnesium that coats small 120V water inlet solenoids, causing erratic ice dispensing and slow valve shutoffs.

Solution: Our technicians flush water supply lines, inspect inline filtration, and install commercial-grade brass-reinforced dual solenoid replacement valves.
