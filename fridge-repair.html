When Do You Need Our Fridge Repair Service?

Modern refrigerators utilize intricate electronics and dual-evaporator airflow channels. If you notice any of these signs, our local fridge repair service team can diagnose the issue on the spot.

01

Fridge Warm But Freezer Cold

A frozen evaporator coil, stuck motorized air damper, or defective defrost timer stops cold air from circulating into the fresh food cabin.

02

Water Leaking Inside or Under Unit

Clogged drain tubes, cracked water reservoir tanks, or faulty dispenser lines lead to ice sheets under crispers and ruined hardwood floors.

03

Loud Humming or Buzzing Noise

Worn evaporator fan motors, failing condenser fan bearings, or vibrating compressor mounts create intolerable noise that signals imminent breakdown.

04

Unit Constantly Running

When dirty condenser coils, torn door gaskets, or failing thermistors prevent the fridge from reaching set temperature, the motor runs continuously.

05

Clicking Relay & No Cooling

If you hear a distinct clicking sound every couple of minutes without cooling, the PTC start relay or run capacitor requires immediate replacement.

06

Freezing Fresh Produce

Defective control boards or miscalibrated temperature sensors cause lettuce and milk to freeze solid in the main refrigeration compartment.
