Common Problems We Solve

Targeted diagnostics for the most frequent mechanical, electrical, and refrigerant malfunctions.

01

Not Cooling or Inconsistent Temps

Caused by clogged condenser coils, failing evaporator fan motors, or defective compressor start relays. We test airflow and electronic thermistors to restore steady temperatures.

02

Water Leaking Under Unit or Drawers

Usually triggered by a frozen or blocked defrost drain tube, cracked water valve, or loose dispenser tubing. We clear obstructions and seal connections to protect flooring.

03

Ice Maker Not Making Ice

Struggling with an ice maker not making ice or cycling cubes? We inspect dual water inlet solenoid valves, frozen fill tube lines, and electronic ejector modules.

04

Excessive Frost & Ice Buildup

Heavy frost covering evaporator coils points to a failed defrost heater, defective bimetal thermostat, or a worn magnetic door gasket that allows warm air infiltration.

05

Unusual Humming, Clicking, or Buzzing

Rattling or loud clicking is commonly traced to worn condenser fan bearings, loose vibration dampeners, or a struggling compressor start capacitor.

06

Constant Running & High Energy Draw

Wondering why is my refrigerator running all the time? If your compressor never cycles off, our technicians inspect dusty condenser coils, failing temperature thermistors, defrost timers, and electronic control boards to restore efficient cooling cycles.
