Heavy-Duty Commercial Cooling Reliability

Commercial refrigeration equipment endures constant door openings, high ambient kitchen temperatures, and non-stop compressor cycling. Unlike residential units, a commercial cooler holding above 40°F puts your business at immediate risk of health inspection fines and lost perishable inventory. Our EPA Section 608 certified technicians understand the urgency of food service operations and deliver swift, accurate diagnosis and industrial-grade repairs across Littleton.

Commercial Refrigeration Issues We Solve Daily

Problem 01

Cabinet Temperature Rising Above 40°F

When commercial reach-ins climb past safe temperatures, food inspection violations follow. We diagnose failed evaporator fan motors, low refrigerant charges, and clogged capillary tubes to quickly restore safe 35°F–38°F ranges.

• Equipment: True, Beverage-Air, Turbo Air
Problem 02

Condensing Unit Overheating & Short Cycling

Kitchen grease and dust clog static condenser fins, causing high head pressure and thermal overload trips. We perform commercial acid degreasing, replace failing condenser fan motors, and test run capacitors.

• Solution: Deep chemical coil wash & capacitor check
Problem 03

Iced Evaporator Coils & Airflow Stoppage

Solid ice blocks across walk-in or reach-in evaporator coils block cold air distribution. We troubleshoot defective defrost termination thermostats, electric defrost heaters, and mechanical defrost time clocks (Grasslin/Paragon).

• Solution: Defrost timer & heater element replacement
Problem 04

Torn Gaskets & Self-Closing Door Failure

Damaged commercial door gaskets and broken torsion spring hinges allow warm kitchen humidity to seep in continuously. We custom-fit magnetic dart gaskets and replace heavy-duty stainless steel hinge cartridges.

• Solution: Custom gasket replacement & torsion hinge adjustment
Problem 05

Thermostatic Expansion Valve (TXV) Failure

A stuck or restricted TXV valve starves the evaporator of refrigerant, causing low suction pressure and severe cooling loss. We test bulb charge pressures, adjust superheat, and braze in new OEM expansion valves.

• Solution: Nitrogen-purged valve brazing & system vacuum
Problem 06

Electronic Digital Controller Errors

Flashing alarm codes (e.g. Carel, Dixell, Danfoss digital controllers) indicating probe errors, high-temp warnings, or defrost malfunctions. We test NTC/PTC sensors, reprogram cycle parameters, and replace control modules.

• Solution: Digital controller programming & probe replacement

How quickly can you dispatch a technician for commercial refrigeration failures?

What commercial refrigeration brands do you service in Littleton?

What are common causes of commercial reach-in cooler failure?

The most frequent issues are clogged condenser coils from grease and dust, failed evaporator fan motors, worn magnetic door gaskets, defective defrost timers, and failing thermostatic expansion valves (TXV).
